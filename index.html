About Crit Happens

Lincoln's friendly tabletop gaming community

Crit Happens is Lincoln's tabletop gaming club, meeting every Monday evening at Washingborough Community Centre. We're a volunteer-led, community-run club open to anyone who loves tabletop games — from seasoned tournament veterans to people who've never rolled a D20 in their life.

Whether you're into painting miniatures, running epic D&D campaigns, or just want to play a few board games with friendly people, there's a place for you at our table.

What We Play

Our members play a huge range of games. On any given Monday you might find a 40K battle unfolding on one table, a D&D dungeon crawl at another, and a Ticket to Ride tournament in the corner. We particularly enjoy Warhammer 40,000, Dropfleet Commander, Pathfinder, and Bolt Action — but we're always open to new games.

New to Tabletop Gaming?

Don't worry if you've never played before. We love introducing new players to the hobby. Your first session is completely free — just get in touch via Discord or the contact form and let us know you're coming. We'll make sure someone's there to show you the ropes.

Teen Sessions

We run dedicated sessions for younger players in a safe, supervised environment. These sessions are designed to be fun and welcoming for teens who want to get into the hobby. Get in touch for more information.

🛡 Safeguarding

The safety and wellbeing of all our members — especially younger players — is our top priority. We have a safeguarding policy in place and all committee members are CRB checked. If you have any concerns, please speak to a committee member or contact us directly.
